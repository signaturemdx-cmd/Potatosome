Irritated skin and a weak barrier feed each other.

Inflammation breaks the barrier

Every flare-up leaves skin a little weaker than before.

A weak barrier lets irritants in

Moisture escapes, triggers get through, and skin reacts again.

The loop never settles

That cycle is why sensitive, chronically inflamed skin is so hard to calm. Fixing only one side is not enough.

Calm the inflammation. Rebuild the barrier. At the same time.

Cosmetic benefits already shown in human tests today. Our research direction: chronic inflammatory skin conditions, where calming and rebuilding have to happen together.

Calms
Less inflammation

Lowers key inflammatory signals (TNF-α, IL-6) in skin cell models, including UV-stressed cells.

Rebuilds
A stronger barrier

Raises the genes that build the skin barrier and hold in water (FLG, TGM1, AQP3, HAS) in skin cells.
